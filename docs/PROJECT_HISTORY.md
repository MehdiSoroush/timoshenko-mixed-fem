# Research provenance

The mixed finite element investigation of Timoshenko beams originated in a **2017 graduate computational mechanics study** (Tir 1396), including static and dynamic MATLAB calculations and reported Abaqus comparisons.

In **November 2026**, the model was reconstructed in Python and extended with an independently assembled displacement–rotation finite element reference, analytical and modal benchmarks, transient comparisons, stress recovery, automated testing, and regenerated figures.

The archival coursework report and corresponding Abaqus numerical datasets are not distributed. The repository's numerical outputs are generated by the 2026 reconstruction and distinguished from the original study.
